    Chess Guide & Dashboard

    Everything you need to know about chess and how to play

    Goal of the Game

    The ultimate objective in chess is to checkmate the opponent's king. This happens when the opponent's king is under immediate attack (in check) and has no legal escape.

    The Board & Setup

    Chess is played on an 8×8 grid with 64 alternating dark and light squares. Horizontal rows are called ranks (1–8) and vertical columns are files (a–h).

    Turn Order & Clock

    White always moves first. Players take turns moving one piece per turn. You cannot skip or pass a turn, and you can never move into or remain in check.

    Capturing Pieces

    A piece captures an enemy piece by landing on its square. The captured piece is permanently removed from the board into the captured tray. The King is never captured.

    ♔ King

    Moves: 1 square in any direction (horizontal, vertical, diagonal).

    Role: The most crucial piece. Must be defended at all costs. Can participate in castling with an unmoved rook.

    ♕ Queen

    Moves: Any number of empty squares horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.

    Role: The most powerful attacking piece on the board, combining the powers of a rook and bishop.

    ♖ Rook

    Moves: Any number of empty squares in a straight line along ranks (rows) or files (columns).

    Role: Dominates open files and coordinates with the king for castling.

    ♗ Bishop

    Moves: Any number of empty squares diagonally.

    Role: Stays on squares of its starting color for the entire match (light-squared or dark-squared).

    ♘ Knight

    Moves: In an "L-shape": two squares in one direction and one square perpendicular.

    Special: The only piece that can jump over other friendly or enemy pieces!

    ♙ Pawn

    Moves: 1 square forward (or 2 squares forward on its very first move). Captures 1 square diagonally forward.

    Special: Cannot move backward. Promotes upon reaching the opposite end of the board.

    Check & Checkmate

    • Check: When a king is attacked. The player must escape check immediately by moving the king, blocking the attack, or capturing the attacker.
    • Checkmate: When the king is in check and cannot escape. Checkmate immediately concludes the match with a win!

    Castling (Rokade)

    A special joint move where the king moves 2 squares toward a rook, and the rook hops over to the square beside the king.

    • Neither the king nor the rook has moved yet.
    • All squares between them must be empty.
    • The king cannot castle out of, through, or into check.

    Pawn Promotion

    When a pawn reaches the furthest rank on the board (rank 8 for White, rank 1 for Black), it immediately promotes into a Queen, Rook, Bishop, or Knight. A popup dialog will appear for you to choose your promoted piece.

    How to Move

    Click on any piece of your color to select it — dots will appear highlighting all legal destination squares. Click an empty highlighted square or enemy piece to complete your move. You can also drag and drop pieces directly.

    Change Pieces

    Use the Change pieces button in the action panel to switch dynamically between the custom hand-drawn pieces and the classic vector SVG chess pieces at any time without resetting the game.

    Themes & Rotation

    • Change theme: Cycles through different board palettes (Red & Dark, Pastel Pink, Cyan Blue, Forest Green).
    • Rotate board: Flips the board 180° for White or Black perspective.

    Scoreboard & New Game

    • Scoreboard: Keeps track of wins for White and Black across your play sessions.
    • Move history: Logs every move with piece icons and coordinates (e.g. e2 → e4).
    • New game: Clears the board and starts a fresh match.
